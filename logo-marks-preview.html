Mark options, seventh roundFour ways of landing between the original Deco fleur and the full fleur-de-lis. Each is shown in place under the real header (left) and at print scale (right).
1Kite centre, curled sides
Between Us
Everything on the table

The centre petal, band and triangle foot are the Deco fleur as it was. Only the side petals are new: the curled, hooked petals of the round-six fleur, drawn with circular arcs.

The cleanest split between the two: straight centre, curved sides.

2Fan with curled outsides
Between Us
Everything on the table

The original fan of five kites, but the outer two kites are replaced by the curled petals, and the two inner kites are made smaller to leave room. Band and foot as before.

The busiest of the four, since it keeps more of the fan.

3Angular fleur
Between Us
Everything on the table

Every edge stays straight, but the side petals are drawn with the fleur's sweep and hook, so the outline is a fleur even without curves. Centre kite, band and triangle foot as in the original.

Fully faceted; none of the curved language.

4Open strokes
Between Us
Everything on the table

The centre kite is closed, but each side petal is drawn as an open sweeping line that ends in a small curl, with a short second line inside it. Band and foot as before.

The lightest and most calligraphic. Petals are lines, not shapes, so it has the least weight.
