Mark options, sixth roundFour fleur-de-lis shapes with the real fleur structure and curved side petals, each given its Deco character in a different way. Each is shown in place under the real header (left) and at print scale (right).
1Compass arcs
Between Us
Everything on the table

The classical fleur shape built only from circular arcs, as if drawn with a compass: pointed centre petal, side petals curling out and back in, band, tails and bud. The precise, geometric kind of curve.

Curves are exact, so it looks drafted, not hand-drawn.

2Inline
Between Us
Everything on the table

The classical fleur with a second, thinner line running inside each petal and the bud, the engraved double-line look common in Deco lettering and signage.

The most lines of the set, and the inner lines are the first thing to fill in at header size.

3Faceted and curved
Between Us
Everything on the table

The centre petal and bud are faceted with straight edges, as in your Deco fleur. The side petals keep their curl. Sharp centre, soft sides.

The only one that mixes the two languages.

4Banded
Between Us
Everything on the table

Classical petals with a triple band across, and under it the tails and bud are replaced by nested chevrons. The stepped, repeated line is the Deco part.

The tails and bud are replaced, so below the band it is the furthest from the classic shape.
