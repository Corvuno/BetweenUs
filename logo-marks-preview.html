Mark options, second roundTen marks, two for each of the first five feelings. Each is shown in place under the real header (left) and at print scale (right). Each one says what feeling it is built to carry and how.

Closeness

1Leaning arcs
Between Us
Everything on the table

Two pairs of arcs lean in toward each other and stop just short of touching, with one small diamond held in the gap at the top. The feeling is two things drawn together, not yet joined.

Reads as an arch with a keystone missing. More upright and tall than the others, so it sits narrow in the header.

2Held between
Between Us
Everything on the table

Two pairs of curved brackets facing each other with a single diamond held between them. The title is literally "Between Us"; this is the only one where the middle is the subject.

The brackets echo the drawn corner brackets on the question card. The most direct reading of the name, and the quietest of the ten.

Candour

3Opened
Between Us
Everything on the table

A diamond opened along a seam, the halves drawn apart with a small light left where it parted. The feeling is that something closed has just been let open.

Keeps the diamond shape the header already has, so it is the smallest change from today.

4Aperture
Between Us
Everything on the table

An octagonal iris, eight blades around a small opening. The feeling is the moment of letting in light, or of being seen.

The most technical of the ten, closest to the "techno" half of the brief. Some people will read it as a camera.

Anticipation

5About to land
Between Us
Everything on the table

A ring left open at the top, with a diamond hovering above the gap. The feeling is the half-second before the last piece falls in, or a card is turned.

Reads as a pause, not an arrival. The only mark with a shape that is deliberately incomplete.

6Hourglass
Between Us
Everything on the table

Two triangles tip to tip between two bars, with the lower one beginning to fill. The feeling is time held, and about to run.

The most literal object in the set, and the busiest at small size because of the two bars.

Warmth

7Glow
Between Us
Everything on the table

A flame-shaped diamond with three arcs of light around it, each fainter than the last. The feeling is a lamp on a table, light that spreads to whoever is near.

Uses fading outer arcs, which can dim into the background at small size.

8Flame
Between Us
Everything on the table

Two nested flame shapes, one inside the other. The feeling is a candle or hearth.

The simplest and most direct of the warm marks, and the most literal: it is a recognisable object, not an abstraction.

Playfulness

9Twirl
Between Us
Everything on the table

Three squares turned a little further each time, so they fan out into a star. The feeling is something set spinning.

Strong silhouette at print scale, but at the real header size the three squares merge into a small blob. It shares a turning idea with the Twist mode without depicting it.

10Pop
Between Us
Everything on the table

A diamond with one edge missing and a small diamond that has just flown off its corner. The feeling is a spark leaving, light and quick.

Keeps the diamond shape the header already has. I avoided a four-point star, because the ✦ is already used for After Dark and as the favicon.
