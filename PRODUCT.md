## Platform

web

## Users

Anyone curious about people: friends, colleagues, dates, strangers. Played in a queue, in a car on
the way home, at work introductions and team-building, with one friend or six, on dates. Also a
first visitor looking through it on their own.

## Product Purpose

A way into another person. A question card game that lets a conversation go where it rarely gets to
on its own, in steps. What it leaves behind: surprise at someone else, sometimes at yourself, and the
pause before an answer.

## Operating Context

You open it and draw. Everything beyond the draw (presets, chapters, categories, Twist, the ways to
play) is there to discover once you're curious. Phone and computer, often passed round a table.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The card screen focuses on the question, and the question alone, plus the controls needed to play.
- Everything else is hidden or layered.
- No card other than the current one is on screen (Three's three cards are the exception).
- An element exists on phone, computer and Spotlight alike, or not at all.
- Card text lives in `questions.js` and is never changed without the owner approving the exact words.

## Brand Commitments

- The name Between Us, its title and the graphic under it.
- "Draw" is the verb for every card action; no gambling or card-table words.
- Fixed in the look: the title font, the question font, the category header above the question, the
  card's shape and its top line in the category colour (`DESIGN.md`).

## Product Principles

- The question is the point of every screen.
- Discovery over instruction: the draw first, everything else when curious.
- One app on every device, not a set of separate screens.
