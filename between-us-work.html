How to play

This isn't a quiz. It's a door, left slightly open.

Most conversations stay on the surface because nothing ever quite gives them permission to go deeper. Between Us is built to do that — a deck of questions for the kind of talk that doesn't usually happen on its own, whether it's two people or a full table.

Draw a card and read it out loud. Answer it yourself, or hand it to someone else. There's nothing to win and nothing to get right. Pass if a question doesn't land — no explanation needed.

Gestures

Tap the card or swipe left for the next question; swipe right to go back. Swipe down on a card to skip it — you get a moment to undo. Tap ★ to keep a favourite. When a hand runs out, tap the button to draw more. Reshuffle re-deals the deck in the same order-mode; the arrows beside the mode name change how it is ordered.

Modes

Each mode sets the table for a situation — who's in the room decides what's on and what's within reach. Dimmed categories aren't part of that room. Work and Family lock After Dark entirely; everywhere else it's yours to switch.

Categories

The full list of categories sits under the presets. Tap one to add it to the mix, tap again to remove it. Anything marked ✦ is adult territory and only appears once After Dark is on.

Shuffle modes

Wild: fully random. Flow: eases in light and gradually deepens. Deep: leans toward harder, more vulnerable cards. Spiced: normal mix with ✦ cards woven in. Heat: leans into intensity. Gentle: keeps things light. Breadth: cycles one card from every active category. Arc: builds a shape — warm start, deep questions, a cool-down, then the pattern repeats. Works well even for a single 5-card hand.

After Dark

Makes the adult and intimate categories (marked ✦) selectable. They are never part of a deck until you switch them on yourself.
