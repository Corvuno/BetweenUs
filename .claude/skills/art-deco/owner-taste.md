# What the owner likes

The second layer of the `art-deco` skill. `SKILL.md` is Deco in general; this is the owner's own
taste, within Deco and beyond it, from their four moodboards and their own words. Quotes are the
owner's; everything else is the Keeper's reading of them, and a reading is a starting point to
check, not a law. `taste.md` holds what the owner looks for in any design; this file is the
looks side of it, made concrete.

## The four boards

The boards are in `.claude/keeper/references/moodboards/`. The owner made them for the one
design language of the whole app, and said with them: "The current intro screens I like. The
current elements I like."

**1. Lines** (`1-lines.jpg`): divider sets. Two dark plates of gold dividers with matching corner
pieces (top right and bottom left), a column of black fan dividers on white, and a sheet of small
gold dividers.
- What they share: bundles of fine lines that bend round the centre and become the shape that
  holds it, so the line and the emblem are one woven thing; faint lines follow the same contour as
  the bold ones; rings, capsules, hexagons and dots as terminals and centres; a fan inside a
  diamond.
- The owner: the app's top bar should be rounder and come "from the two divider plates";
  dividers are a slim and a very slim version of the top bar, the same family, "an element that
  returns".

**2. Things** (`2-things.jpg`): green marble fan scales with gold rims; a stepped frosted-glass
sconce in a brass holder; a white streamline house in black and white; a pattern of gold, slate
and navy fans, discs and leaves in overlapping planes.
- The owner on the house: bold and subdued in one piece, unapologetic. On the lamp: low weight.
- What they share: colour and tone in flat planes with metal edges; overlap and layering (glass
  in front of glass, scale over scale, fan in front of disc); round shapes built with the compass;
  three tones giving depth without any light effect.

**3. Card frames** (`3-card-frames.jpg`): a black landscape frame of gold bundles crossing on the
diagonal, with circles, a lattice diamond and lines ending on dots; a navy frame of beaded bands
and faceted side points; a slate frame of crossing diagonals with solid gold triangles; a row of
four small frames with rounded and cut corners, fans and notches at the edges.
- The owner likes the top black frame and the bottom row best, and wants the richness of a card
  back (elements inside elements) combined with Deco frames.

**4. Card backs** (`4-card-backs.jpg`): the gold Anubis back (winged figures and an eye on a
mirrored axis), an ornate orange scroll back, a black and red owl back, a blue medallion back.
Two of these are not Deco at all; the board is about card backs, not about the style.
- The owner: what makes them is three colour layers (grey, black and gold, as in the Anubis and
  blue backs) giving depth; a strong general shape from afar ("gold in your face"), then the
  words, then tiny details up close. That scale from far to close is wanted for the whole app.
  The board is input for the later card back, which is a topic of its own.

## What the owner looks for, in their words

- **Bold next to fine.** "There should be things that are loud and like clear, bold, and things
  that are muted and small." An ornate, tiny frame can live around the card because it is small,
  "and then other things have the space to be bolder again." All thin and muted is the failure:
  "flimsy and thin and muted… It doesn't have a hierarchy."
- **One thought, done everywhere.** Cohesion is not ornament round the card ("You just threw
  stuff around the card and called it a day"). It is one grammar for the whole interface: the
  same few shapes, lines, headings, rows and tap signals on every screen, phone and computer
  alike. Fewer different things. "It IS the cohesion: one thought, done everywhere."
- **Something holds it.** On a computer the app should read as one application, "not this is a
  settings menu, this is an element". Nothing floats; the card is tied into something.
- **Round shapes.** "I like round shapes. I love round shapes", and "you're quite bad at them".
  Rings, discs, arcs, capsule ends, rounded corners, next to the straight and the stepped.
- **Geometric over curvy.** "I'm more into geometric lines than curve." Curves come from the
  compass, not freehand.
- **Layers.** "Do layers. Not everything with hard lines. Layers. Again, layers." Depth comes from
  planes and tones stacked on each other (the three colour layers of the card backs), never from
  light.
- **Subtle.** The small glints added to the old start backs, placed where they help, were the
  owner's idea of subtle; the heavier attempts became "ham-handed". That is a direction, not
  something to copy: "Don't copy them."
- **Lines that lead the eye**, with a job and a direction; never lines whose only job is to flank
  something ("the standard deco flair for AI").
- **Corners and parts that travel.** A card border that is cut up and bordered, with corners
  useful elsewhere; a top element that is good on its own, then layered, then abstracted into
  smaller pieces seen elsewhere.

## What is never wanted

Each of these protects something the owner does want.

- **No glow, in any form, and no soft drop shadows.** The owner calls both tells of AI design.
  Depth comes from layers and tone (see above).
- **No buttons.** "I don't want buttons, I don't do buttons": no outlined boxes around a control,
  and no big filled bars either. Something tappable shows it through colour, symbol, placement and
  line work, unmistakably, without a box (`look.md`, "Controls").
- **Nothing random.** "Never random. Always knowing what's happening." No seeded or scattered
  placement, not even when the picture looks the same each time: every mark sits by a rule that
  can be said.
- **No patches.** Feedback on a part means drawing it again from its idea; a stub line, a
  recolour or a swapped glyph keeps the structure that was criticised ("the most lazy fix I have
  ever seen").
- **No added glyphs.** Small shapes stuck onto a line as decoration read as random add-ons; a
  shape is part of the line's construction or it isn't there.
- **Not timid.** Hairline-only parts and plain right-angle corners read as not Deco.
- **Federo for every tappable** "works" but "is not it"; better options exist.

## What stays fixed

The card's physical proportions, the card's metal top line in the category colour (it is one of
the two dividers), the three to four fonts, the logo (two overlapping diamonds, which mean
"between us", at the centre of the bar under the name), and Next card. Whatever else changes
should grow out of these, so the app keeps its own face.

## How a round goes for this owner

- Questions about what they want before any picture; never "shall I?".
- Three or four sound options plus one rethink that is really out there and still works; never
  the barest version.
- No pick or favourite from the Keeper or the designer when the owner chooses.
- One phone per option, today's version first in the same crop and moment.
- After a no, a new idea, never an iteration.
