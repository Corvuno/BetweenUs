# Writer test key — round 3 (2026-10-22)

Do not open or mention in front of the owner until every card is picked. A = normal method, B = method + humanizer.

## Connect 1
1. A — Which detail did someone remember about you that told you, out of nowhere, that you mattered to them?
2. B — Who remembered something about you that you'd forgotten you ever told them?
3. A — When did something small you did for someone turn out to matter far more to them than you thought?
4. B — What's the smallest thing you've done for someone that they still bring up years later?
5. A — Tell about a time you tried to show someone they mattered and it landed completely wrong.
6. B — Who was hard to show you cared about, and how did you finally get it across?

## Connect 2
1. B — Where did you have your best conversation with someone whose name you never learned?
2. A — What do you ask people you've just met to get past the weather?
3. A+B — What did a stranger say that turned small talk into a real conversation?
4. B — When did you tell a stranger something you hadn't told the people close to you?
5. A — Where were you when a conversation with someone you'd just met went far deeper than either of you planned?

## Connect 6
1. B — Who do you message nearly every day, and what do you send each other?
2. A — When did how fast or slow you replied get read as something you never meant?
3. A — Who is the one person you still call rather than text?
4. A — What does a typical voice note from you sound like?
5. B — When your phone rings with a call instead of a message, what's the first thing you think?
6. B — Whose voice notes do you listen to all the way through, and whose do you speed up?

## Connect 8
1. B — When did you finally talk to someone you'd been nodding at for months?
2. B — Who do you see almost every day without knowing their name, and what have you decided about their life?
3. B — Which regular in your day would you notice first if they stopped turning up?
4. A — What story have you made up about someone you see every day but have never really spoken to?
5. A — Tell about the day you finally spoke properly to someone you'd only ever nodded at.
6. A — Whose face from your daily routine would you genuinely miss if it vanished tomorrow?

## Connect 10
1. A — Who surprised you most when you saw them again after a long time?
2. B — What did you write to break years of silence with someone, and what came back?
3. B — Who did you pick straight back up with after years apart, as if no time had passed?
4. A — Who did you meet again after years apart, and what was the first thing that felt exactly the same?
5. B — Tell about seeing someone again after years and finding they'd become a stranger.
6. A — What would your first message be to someone you lost touch with years ago and still think of?

## Friendship 6
1. A — Which friend did you make in the unlikeliest place?
2. B — What's the first thing you remember about your closest friend?
3. A — Which of your closest friends did you not like at first?
4. B — Who introduced you to one of your closest friends, and do they get the credit?
5. B — Which of your friendships started by pure accident?
6. A — How did you meet your closest friend, and how close did you come to never meeting at all?

## Friendship 9
1. B — What's your friends' group chat called, and how did it get that name?
2. A+B — In your group of friends, who organises everything, and what would happen if they stopped?
3. A — What did your group chat last get completely carried away about?
4. A — Which role do you play in your group of friends that you never actually signed up for?
5. B — Which role in your group of friends is always yours, whether you want it or not?

## Friendship 10
1. A — Which standing arrangement with a friend has quietly lasted the longest, and how did it start?
2. B — Which friend has a standing date with you, and how did it start?
3. B — What do you and one particular friend do that would look odd to anyone watching?
4. B — Which word or phrase means something only to you and one friend?
5. A — What do you always do with one particular friend that you'd never do with anyone else?
6. A — What's a word or phrase that means something only to you and one friend?

## Friendship 12
1. B — What do you only talk about with the friends who knew you at eighteen?
2. B — What story does an old friend tell about you that you'd rather they didn't?
3. A — What happened when an old friend finally met the people in your life now?
4. A — What still holds you together with the friend who has known you the longest?
5. B — Which old friend is still the best at telling when you're putting it on?
6. A — What does your oldest friend know about you that nobody you've met since would believe?

## Romance 4
1. B — What did you once do on a first date that you'd never do now?
2. B — Tell about the first date you wanted to leave within ten minutes.
3. A — Where do you take someone on a first date when you want to find out who they really are?
4. B — What's the best first date you've been on, and how much of it was planned?
5. A — What's the worst first date you still stayed until the end of?
6. A — Tell about your best first date, and the moment you knew it was going well.

## Romance 5
1. A — Can you really stay friends with an ex, and what's your own track record?
2. B — Who's the one that got away, and would it have worked?
3. A+B — Tell about running into an ex when you least expected it.
4. B — Which ex could you still have a drink with, and which one could you not?
5. A — Which ex do you still wonder about now and then?

## Romance 9
1. B — How do you mark an anniversary, and has one ever slipped your mind?
2. A — Is a wedding for the couple, or for everyone else?
3. A — Which wedding you went to do you still talk about, and why?
4. B — Would you want to be proposed to in front of a crowd?
5. B — What's the best wedding speech you've ever heard, or the worst?
6. A — What's the most public proposal you've ever witnessed, and how did it feel to watch?

## Family 1
1. A — Where do you end up sitting at a family birthday, and who do you end up talking to?
2. B — What happens at every family birthday, without fail?
3. B — Who runs your family group chat, and what does it mostly contain?
4. B — Which seat do you end up in at a family dinner, and who's always next to you?
5. A — What happens at every family get-together, without fail?
6. A — Which family holiday tradition would you defend, and which would you quietly drop?

## Family 3
1. A — Who in your family can't keep anything to themselves, and what did they give away?
2. B — Who in your family hears everything first, and who's always the last to know?
3. A — What big news of yours did your family find out in the wrong order?
4. B — What news did you once have to break to your family, and who did you tell first?
5. B — What's the last piece of family news you found out by accident?
6. A — In your family, who hears the news first, and who always hears it last?

## Family 10
1. A+B — When did you last look after someone in your family, or let them look after you?
2. A — Who in your family is the one everyone calls when something goes wrong?
3. A — When did money last change hands in your family, and did anyone keep count?
4. B — Who in your family do you call when something needs fixing?
5. B — Who in your family pays when you eat out together, and was that ever discussed?
