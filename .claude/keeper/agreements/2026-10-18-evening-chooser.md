# Agreement — the evening chooser (2026-10-18)

Approved by the owner 2026-10-18 ("show me some options" after the draft).

## Why
The sheet headed "What kind of evening?" feels clunky and unclear. Two jobs share one long
scroll: picking an evening, and mixing your own categories. Explore categories was folded
away to fix the layering; it didn't work well. The owner and friends regularly mix
categories across chapters (e.g. Carnal, Past, Connect, Deep), on the mood of the moment.
The owner never uses the Depth and Focus sliders.

History: mix-and-match used to be its own tab. It confused people because the tabs had
fancy names, felt like separate paths, and sat on top.

## C) How it works
- The sheet opens on the evening only: Start from (presets), Chapters, Shuffle and Cards, Draw.
- Depth and Focus sliders go from the sheet. Each preset keeps setting them underneath
  (ui.js preset intent values), so presets play exactly as now.
- "Explore categories" becomes one plainly named door at the bottom. It opens its own view:
  every category from every chapter side by side, All / None, Fixed Sets (Colbert, The 36).
  A clear way back to the evening.
- The door is an extra, never a second path: no fancy name, not on top.

## B) Looks
Mockup rounds, each on its own link, the current sheet next to the options, one real
rethink per round. The builder builds only the chosen mockup. Door wording and how the mix
view opens are shown as options.

## A) Deck
Nothing changes.

## Does not change
Presets and what they select, chapter names and lines, Save, the main screen, the
time-of-day title, the cards. Draw without opening the sheet plays as today.

## Done when
The evening part fits one phone screen without scrolling; the mix door can't be mistaken
for a second path; the owner picked a mockup and it's built and live.
