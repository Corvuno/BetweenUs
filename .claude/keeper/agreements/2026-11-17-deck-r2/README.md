# The Deck, round 2: three different looks (#183)

Owner on round 1: "The point was to give the categories so they would not overwhelm people the
first time they entered the menu. And usually it's in its with-description state for me." And:
"Now do it again. (Different looks. Not just updated)". So round 1's sketches were the same sheet
rearranged, and its second one showed every category on entry, against the reason the levels fold.

What every look in this round keeps: on entry the categories are folded away (ways to start and
six levels, no more); the owner's usual state is a level open with the descriptions on, so each
look is drawn in both moments, beside the live sheet in the same two moments (Balanced deck,
Beneath the Surface open, phone). Shuffle, Cards, Three, Wildcards and the fixed sets are left
as they are at the foot.

1. **Steps.** The six levels as one stepped figure that narrows downwards and closes in a point;
   double frame, the level's colour as the inner line when in. An open step widens to the sheet.
2. **Doors.** Each level a doorway with stepped shoulders, three to a row; in: colour in the frame
   and a line of light under the door (shorter when partly in). A door's room opens under its row.
   After the start card's "Each question is a door, left slightly open."
3. **The line.** No frames: large level names with their line and dots along one metal line down
   the left; the stretch beside a level fills with its colour when in.

The Keeper's own pick: 2.
