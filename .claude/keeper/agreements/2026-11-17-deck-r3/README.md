# The Deck, round 3: the line in metal, and a scale of how deep (#183)

Owner on round 2: the groups are not a hierarchy. Arrive, The Surface, Beneath the Surface and
Into the Deep go from light to deep (starting a conversation, going deeper, deeper still); Between
Us (togetherness) and After Dark (sexuality, spice) are outliers. Doors: too much ornament. Liked
the line ("also because I like the current design"), with the colours more metallic: easier on
the eyes. The category dots mean nothing: nobody knows the category colours, the owner included;
the state must be said another way. Players do use the categories (screenshots show picked ones),
so they are folded by default, one step away, not far out of the way. Asked for one version of
the line and one or more other options.

1. **The line, in metal.** Four depth levels down one metal line; the stretch beside a level fills
   with its colour as metal (part of the way when partly in), the name in the same metal. State in
   words under each: All in, Not in, Without <names> (two or fewer out), Only <names> (two or fewer
   in), else "n of m". Between Us and After Dark side by side under the line, with a short metal
   bar on top. A celadon arrow opens the categories with descriptions.
2. **How deep, and two switches.** The four levels as one horizontal scale, Light to Deep, the
   played stretch lit in gold metal; arrows under level names open the categories under the scale.
   Between Us and After Dark as Off / On switches with the state word when part is in.

The Keeper's own pick: 2, for saying the deck the way the owner described it.

`looks.html` holds both in two moments; `render.mjs` takes the pictures.
