# Under the card: what each control is (#113, before round 3)

Written after round 2, when the owner asked: "Did you look what the functions are, what you are
editing, and what the philosophy of the application is?" and said of G, I and K: "take element,
paste elsewhere". Rounds 1 and 2 moved the same parts around without this.

## The philosophy (vision.md, Ways to play)
- The phone is the deck. It is held by one person and passed on ("Pass the deck", "hand the
  deck on", "then the next person draws"). "It's a door, left slightly open."
- The screen focuses on the question alone; the controls needed to play are shown, the rest is
  layered. Draw is the one clear main action. Three and Spotlight belong right next to the draw.

## What each control is (from the code)
| Control | Kind | What it does | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| Draw / Next card | the action | draws the next card. "Draw Card" before the first, "Next Card", "End of hand" on the last; at the end of a hand "Tap to flip card", then "Hold to continue" | every turn |
| Swipe / tap on the card | the same action | next and back without the button | every turn |
| Back | correction | the card before; dead before the first card | rare |
| Three | a way of drawing, stays on | every draw offers three cards; turning it on draws at once; off puts the planned card back | set once per evening, or never |
| Twist | acts on this card | adds a sentence to the shown card; tap again removes it; dead before the first card, at hand end, on wildcards | now and then, per card |
| Star | acts on this card | favourite; already on the card | now and then |
| Spotlight | a way of showing, stays on | the question fills the screen, for the table; can turn sideways | per evening or per card |
| Count + coloured line | status | where you are in the hand; hands are 5, 10, 20 or All | always |
| Menu | leaving the game | The Deck, Start over, Played, Favourites, Ways to play | rare |
| Change / Save | end of hand only | replace the play row at Intermezzo | once per hand |

## What this explains
Today's row treats Three, Spotlight and Twist as three of a kind. They aren't: Three changes how
you draw (it belongs with Draw), Twist changes the card (it belongs with the card, like the star),
Spotlight changes how the card is shown. Three different things dressed as equals, next to Back
and Menu (a correction and an exit) drawn as boxes, is a large part of why it reads incoherent.
Moving those same parts to new places (rounds 1 and 2) can't fix that.

1. Where it's played differs: at a table, in a line for a rave, in a car; then one person holds it
