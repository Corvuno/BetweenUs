Wildcard, round 1

A wildcard is a special card in a hand (never the first, at most one) that tells the player to ask their own question. Its title is "Wildcard"; the text shown is "Ask anyone here the question you've been saving." Each picture is the real app with the wildcard put on the third card of a hand. Current is first. Tap a picture to see it larger.
