# Start card back, round 7: two sets (#195)

Shown: https://claude.ai/artifact/6BTZUhRu5TMMpk4XsE6UMR (the live start card on a phone, still and
arriving).

Owner on round 6: "You still gave it the shape of the eye, now the shape of the new card... Also: The
alternative version: look at the refeneces I mentionsed and show me options that utilize that
knowlage" and "So two sets".

Round 6 made every arch a whole circle, which is the Eye's shape. Lesson: "continue" means a line runs
on through the crossing it meets, not that the figure is completed into the reference's geometry.

Set 1 keeps round 4's 3 and round 5's b exactly; each arch carries on straight along its own direction
past where it used to stop (round 4: where it met its mirror at the sides; round 5: its own upright),
through the crossing to the frame. Petrol only, sparkle layer under. Exports `straightColumns` (1a),
`straightTurns` (1b), `straightDoors` (1c).

Set 2 builds on the Keeper's read of what makes the reference decks a card back: drawn for the
rectangle (border, designed corners and ends, nothing cut by the edge), zones with their own
treatment, one object on the centre line, planned density; every line ends on something. Exports
`techno` (2a, the techno card), `medallion` (2b, the mystic game's box), `wings` (2c, the Egyptian
backs), `cutAreas` (2d, the outlier: the tarot box's "cut of areas that get their own design").

The Keeper's verdict with the round: 1b in set 1, 2b in set 2; set 2 reads more as a card than any
earlier round. Nothing here is chosen.
