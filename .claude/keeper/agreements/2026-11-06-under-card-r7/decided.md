# Under the card (#113): decided, open, proposals

Every round page for this topic opens with this file. Only the owner's own words move a line to
"Decided". Earlier rounds: 2026-11-05-under-card-r1 … r6, and 2026-11-06-under-card-r7/thinking.md.

## Decided (owner's words)

- **One standard, scaled.** Designed for a phone the size of a Xiaomi 14 Ultra (412 × 915), scaled
  down on smaller phones: "Design for a phone like the Xiaomi 14 Ultra and scale the interface down
  when needed. I don't want 10 versions. Should be a standard." (2026-11-05)
- **What the area must do:** "indicate the card count, next, previous, menu, spotlight (and maybe
  twist/three)" (2026-11-05). Later: Back "Remove it, might not miss it at all" (2026-11-05), so
  "previous" may be a swipe only.
- **Spotlight stays in this area:** "Three and twist can also be hidden elsewhere if needed.
  Spotlight should stay." (2026-11-05)
- **Three and Twist leave this space:** "Somewhere not that space." Spotlight is not a place to
  hide them: "spotlight is a view of the card, not a menu, nothing can ever 'hide' in spotlight
  (the controls can be placed there as well for some things)." (2026-11-05)
- **Hints stay in some form:** "Yea, the hints help. How you display them, I don't know. They are
  removed after the first click." (2026-11-05)
- **The card is for reading only:** "Card is for reading questions. NOT ANYTHING ELSE. NO
  interaction elements on the card." (2026-11-05)
- **The card keeps a real card's proportions:** "card dimensions are a real card. With reason. So
  keep it that way. That's a design constraint." (2026-11-05)
- **No small diamonds outside After Dark:** "ditch the small diamond, that's After Dark. Don't use
  it elsewhere. (it can be used in buttons, not as icon)" (2026-11-05)
- **Centre and balance:** "I like centre compositions… I also like balance." (2026-11-05)
- **Situational controls, without gaps:** "that might actually be better, as long as it does not
  give empty spots and keeps the symmetry balance." (2026-11-05)
- **The problem is fundamental, not arrangement:** "It does not match the rest of the aesthetic.
  Rearranging what is is not going to change that. It's the fundamentals… Something not Art Deco
  enough." (2026-11-05) and "not a fundamental 'what does it do, how does it work and what does the
  player interact with'" (2026-11-06)
- **Diagnosis (critique.md in r6) "mostly fits"** (2026-11-05).
- **Menu layers:** "menu layers have never been a hit. Especially if they have no reason to be
  layered." (2026-11-06)
- **Sharing:** "For now screenshot works fine." (2026-11-05)
