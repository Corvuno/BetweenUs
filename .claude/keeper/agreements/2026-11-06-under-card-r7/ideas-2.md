# Round 7 ideas, second try (words, before the gate)

Decided and holding: Spotlight stays under the card; Three's setting is in The Deck; Twist leaves this
space (its home is a separate round); nothing on the card; the card keeps its shape; centre and
balance; controls only when usable, no gaps. The owner allows one or two options with the hand as card
backs; the rest must be different ideas. Back, Menu's handle and hints are open: each idea places them
with its own reason.

1. **The hand (cards).** The player draws by touching the rest of the hand, which lies face down
   under the question and gets thinner as you play; the coloured line runs along the stack's edge as
   the hand's progress. Back: the hand gets the card back when you swipe the question back to it,
   because that's where it came from. Menu: from "Selected deck", the label of the box the deck came
   in. Hints: written on the top back of the stack on the first visit, where the eye goes to draw.
2. **The dial.** The player turns a deco radio dial to move through the hand, one notch per question,
   turning back to go back; the numbers around the dial are the count. Spotlight sits at the dial's
   centre, because pressing the knob "turns the sound up" for the table. Menu: from "Selected deck",
   the box. Hints: printed on the dial's face on the first visit, the way a radio's face names its
   stations.
3. **The stage.** The lower part becomes the front edge of a stage with footlights, in the app's own
   words "the question takes the stage": tapping the stage edge calls the next question on; holding it
   raises the lights fully (Spotlight); the footlights that are lit are the hand's progress. Back: a
   swipe back on the stage sends the question off and the previous one on. Menu: from "Selected deck".
   Hints: one line in the stage edge's own lettering on the first visit.
4. **Rethink: the evening is a programme.** Like a printed theatre programme, the hand is listed under
   the question as numbered acts with only the act numbers and the current one marked, no future text;
   the player taps the next act number to go on, any earlier number to go back; the programme is the
   count and the back; Spotlight is "the house lights down" at the programme's foot; Menu from the
   programme's title (the deck's name). Against the overview: next → tap the next act; back → tap an
   earlier act; count → the acts; Spotlight → house lights at the foot; Menu → programme title;
   hints → the programme's subtitle line on the first visit.


## Design-gate verdict (2026-11-06): ideas 1, 2 and 4 pass; 3 blocked
Idea 3 (the stage) is the gold bar with Spotlight in theatre dress: dropped. The gate noted that Menu
"from Selected deck" repeated with the same reason in every idea; for the sketches each idea gets its
own Menu place with its own reason:
- 1 the hand: Menu from "Selected deck", the label of the box the deck came in.
- 2 the dial: Menu is the radio's band switch beside the dial, because it changes what's playing
  (which deck), what has played, what you kept.
- 4 the programme: Menu from the programme's title (the deck's name), as a programme's cover opens it.
