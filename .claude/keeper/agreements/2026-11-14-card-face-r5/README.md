# Card face, round 5: the chosen face (#159)

Owner on round 4: agreed to take the stronger line and leave the bottom stripes ("I agree, And i
like a."), and noticed "the metal thing in the top of the card become less visible".

`card-face.css` is the chosen face, laid over the live app: `vA xD` on `body`, `--cat` on `#card`
(the name's colour), and `<i class="cf"><i></i><i></i><i></i><i></i></i>` in `#card` before
`.card-body-inner`. Its last two blocks give the top line room (the card's edge gold as today,
the frame further in) and set the computer's sizes. The rules for `vB`, `vC`, `xE`, the fan and
`.cf b` are earlier variants that were not chosen and are not built.
