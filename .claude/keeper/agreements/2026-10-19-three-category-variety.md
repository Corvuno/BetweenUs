# Three: options from different categories — 2026-10-19

Owner's wish: Three often shows several options from the same category. With only one or
two categories in the selection that's fine; with more, variety is better. Area: C (how the
app works). A and B don't change.

Cause: in Arc the extras are sorted by nearest depth to the planned card, and every card in a
category shares one depth, so the planned card's own category tends to win.

Rule (owner, 2026-10-19: "Freshness beats variety. Always."):
- Freshness order stays exactly as now: unseen cards first, then cards seen this pass, then
  last hand's sit-outs (unseen before seen).
- Within a freshness tier, prefer cards whose category is not yet among the options (the
  planned card's category included). Arc keeps nearest depth as the order among those.
- A same-category card is used only when a tier holds no fresh card of another category;
  variety never pulls a card from a later (less fresh) tier.
- With one or two categories selected, repeats are fine (follows from the above).

Stays: everything from 2026-10-18-three-late-in-pass.md, the After Dark opener rule, the
sit-out rule, how picking works, the looks.

Done when: with a broad selection, the three options come from three different categories
(checked over many draws, Arc and Wild, fresh pass); late in a pass freshness still wins; a
one-category selection still offers three; tests pass with a new check.
