Category lines, round 1

Issue #85: show each category’s own line in The Deck, so players no longer have to guess what a category does. The lines are the app’s own, word for word, and show whenever a chapter is open. With every chapter closed, The Deck looks as it does today. Today and three options, built from the live app, on a phone sheet and in the PC panel.

Same moment in all four: Beneath the Surface open, seven of its eleven categories picked (Balanced picks all eleven, so Body, Values, Attract and Spirit were switched off to show both looks). Tap a picture to see it full size. After Dark has three of five picked.

3 · Rethink: small cards

The rethink. Each category is a small card from this deck: its own top rail in the category colour, the corner brackets, the name on top, the line below in the card’s italic, laid out as a grid, so opening a chapter feels like looking through a boxed deck. A card you have not picked sits quieter, with a fainter rail. Gives up height: it is the tallest of the four on a phone, and the cards want more room than names do.
