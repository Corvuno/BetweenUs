Notes

  • Every poster is shown full-bleed and with a white margin of 8 mm. The dark art holds its own card frame inside, so it reads either way.
  • Prompt size is the same on all four posters of an option, 76 px (about 20 mm) in A, B and C; D uses the same size in a narrower window, so some prompts take three or four lines.
  • Soft background light: option C only. Navy tint: option C only. The woven card back: option D only.
  • The Be Free symbol is extracted from their sun image (white figure, transparent background), unaltered, 4 cm tall. The source is small, so it prints sharp up to about 4 cm and no larger.
  • The Between Us mark is the app's own card-back markup, unchanged (gold, as with no preset).
  • The ink is the screen render; fonts come from Google Fonts, so open with internet access.
