Owner while round 6 was being shown: "Better: make a few option. Really different once. If you give
me one, ill be shooting it down all day." And: "be more subtle. Your lines are big bold and
unforgiving. Look at the references: they use shades, small and large objects. All in a different
way. Never just a large diamond."

`backs.js` (with `backs-frames.js` for the first): four directions, each built on its own principle,
all in finer lines with softly shaded areas and pieces of more than one size.

- `a-frames`: round 6 made quieter. The window echoes the frame; mitre bundles make four areas;
  the large side diamonds are gone, replaced by a small sunrise from the frame and a row of stones.
- `b-portals`: nested stepped gateways; the window is the innermost; bands alternate fine lines,
  shade and weave; fans in the outer bands and the corners.
- `c-circles`: a great circle from below and one from above; the window is their overlap; rings
  cross into shaded curved cells; a fan and a level bundle at each point.
- `d-streams`: lines enter level and part around the window at forty-five degrees, shaded stripes
  between some, a fan on the window's flats, stones on the centre line.

Nothing here is chosen. How the back arrives at the end of a hand, and the Intermezzo's window, are
not designed in this round.
