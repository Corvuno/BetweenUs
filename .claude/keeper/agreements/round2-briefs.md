### Culture
**App line:** Everything you love that someone else made — from a song to a building
**About:** Your taste, and what it says about you. Everything people make, as taste: buildings,
design, art, music, film, books, games, food, fashion, humour, the internet. What you love, what
leaves you cold, and how your taste formed and changed. Culture is the "what", the tangible thing.
**Lines of thought:** buildings and places that do something to you · design: furniture, objects,
cars, the things you use · sculpture, painting, photography, art in the street · crafts:
handmade things, makers you admire · music, film, series and books · games and sport as a fan ·
food as taste: cuisines, restaurants, what you'd never eat · fashion and style · humour: what
makes you laugh, and who · the internet: creators, memes, rabbit holes · trends you joined, and
trends you refused · concerts, festivals, museums · taste you inherited, and taste you found
yourself · what you'd show someone to explain who you are · things you hated at first and love now
· being moved by something made, as one theme among many · art versus the artist.
**Where it stops:** why free time matters to you is Unwind's · your country and culture are
World's · cooking day to day is Life's · music in your body is Body's · your own house and rooms
are Home's; a building or design as something you admire or hate is Culture's.

### Roots
**App line:** (unchanged) The house you grew up in, and what it's still doing
**About:** Your childhood and where you come from: the people, places and things that made you,
the good and the hard, and what's still with you. Cards must also work for players with few
childhood memories: stories the family tells, things you kept, where the family comes from,
grandparents, words and habits from where you grew up, what people expected of you.
**Lines of thought:** the house and the neighbourhood you grew up in · childhood friends,
brothers, sisters, cousins · school, classmates, the playground · games, toys, outside play ·
holidays and family trips · food at home · rules and punishments, and what you got away with ·
money at home · the music and TV of your childhood · what your parents did for a living ·
traditions, celebrations, birthdays · the village, town or country you come from · moving as a
child · what you were like as a child · your grandparents and their house · the neighbours ·
your first pet · chores you had to do · the language or dialect at home · faith at home ·
the first time away from home · stories your family tells about you · things you kept ·
what's still with you.
**Where it stops:** your family as it is now is Family's · later turning points are Past's ·
where you live now is Home's · the rules you live by now are Values'. Music and TV of your
childhood stay here as childhood; as taste they are Culture's.

### Wish
**App line:** Dreams kept, dreams outgrown
**About:** Wanting: what you want from your life, the dreams you kept and outgrew, the other life
you could have had, plans and the courage they take. Concrete wishes, not reflection on your life.
**Lines of thought:** childhood dreams, and where they went · the plan you keep putting off · the
life you almost lived · what you're saving up for · a wish that came true, and how that felt ·
what you'd learn if time didn't matter · wanting what someone else has · letting a wish go · a
secret ambition · the next five years · what you'd do if you weren't afraid · who you're slowly
becoming · wanting less · a deadline you set yourself · the strangest item on your list · a wish
for the world, not for yourself.
**Where it stops:** daily life is Life's · career ambition is Work's · wanting a person is
Attract's · regret about the past is Past's · meaning is Spirit's.

### Work
**App line:** The job you have, and the ones that got you here
**About:** Working life in full: jobs, colleagues, money and the reality of the day, as well as
ambition and meaning. Study, side jobs and unpaid work count too.
**Lines of thought:** first job, worst job, strangest job · bosses, good and bad · colleagues,
friends at work, the work party · an ordinary working day · mistakes and disasters at work ·
money: first pay, raises, asking for more · fired, quitting, job interviews · work you're proud
of · burnout and balance · ambition, choosing a field · side jobs · what people get wrong about
your job · stopping work, retirement · the job you'd do for free · rituals at work: coffee, Friday
drinks · being out of work · working from home · uniforms and work clothes · dreams about work ·
the job your parents wanted for you · being the boss yourself.
**Where it stops:** fun and hobbies are Unwind's · chores are Life's · a talent you never turned
into a job can stay here.

### Spirit
**App line:** What you believe when no one is asking
**About:** The big questions as you've lived them: belief and doubt, death, meaning, awe, ritual,
luck and fate, and what you hope is true.
**Lines of thought:** what you were raised to believe, and what's left of it · faith, doubt,
changing your mind · death: your own, what you think comes after · close calls · awe, wonder,
feeling small · rituals, prayer, meditation · sacred places · signs, coincidences, luck and fate ·
superstitions · what your own funeral should be like · what you'd ask the universe if you got an
answer · faith talk at the family table · karma · things you can't explain · moments of grace ·
the holy in everyday things · rebirth, the soul.
**Where it stops:** loss and mourning are Grief's · principles are Values' · gratitude to people
is Connect's · how your head works is Mind's.

### Body
**App line:** How you treat the only body you'll get
**About:** Living in a body: what it can and can't do, how it feels, how you treat it, and how
others see it. Health, movement, senses, ageing, appearance. Not sexual.
**Lines of thought:** strength and limits · sport and movement · injuries, scars, the hospital ·
illness and health scares · pain · senses: smell, touch, taste, hearing · getting older, the first
grey hair · appearance: hair, tattoos, piercings, how tall you are · you and the mirror · how you
see your body · what your body tells you: gut feelings, tension · rest and care: massage, sauna ·
hangovers and what you put into it · body and emotion: crying, blushing · your voice · doctors
and medicine · a moment of endurance · cold, heat, hunger · your body after illness, children or
age.
**Where it stops:** sex is After Dark's · the body as attraction is Attract's · food as taste is
Culture's, cooking is Life's.

### Unwind
**App line:** What you do when nothing has to be done
**About:** The why and the feeling of free time: holidays, hobbies, games, fun, rest, and the
things that make life worth it. Culture covers the what.
**Lines of thought:** holidays and travel · weekends, the perfect Sunday · hobbies you kept, and
ones you dropped · making things · fun with friends, nights out · doing nothing · outdoors, the
weather, the seasons, camping · small pleasures · dancing · getting out of your head · a holiday
that went wrong · collections · festivals · a guilty-pleasure pastime · what you do to reset ·
play as an adult.
**Where it stops:** media and taste are Culture's · sport as body is Body's · chores are Life's.
