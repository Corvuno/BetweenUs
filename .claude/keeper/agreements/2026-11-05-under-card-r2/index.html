G · Like Spotlight

Takes the Spotlight layout you liked (arrows in the bottom corners, gold in the middle, small items at the top) and uses it on the main screen, so both screens speak the same language. One centre column: count, Spotlight, Next card. Next sits low, under the thumb, and the tall screen gets used.

The screen splits into two parts with empty space between them. Three and Twist sit at the edges, far apart, so they read as less important than today.

H · F, lighter

Keeps F's symmetry and stepped gold Next and drops the plaque, the diamond buttons and the brackets. Everything hangs off one centre line, close under the card. Spotlight has its own line, with Three and Twist under it as a pair.

Still four layers stacked under the card. The stepped ends are the only deco left, so it may now be too quiet for "bold". Like today, it leaves the bottom of a tall phone empty.

I · D, hint-proof

D's thumb bar in pairs around the gold centre: Back and Menu outside, Three and Twist inside. Each label has room for its hint all the time, so the bar has the same height before and after. Spotlight stands alone under the card, by the question it puts on stage.

Still the most like a regular phone app. Five things in one bar is busy, and the hints are small at that size. Next card is narrower than today.

J · Rethink: the deck in your hand

No row of controls. The rest of the hand lies under the card as a real stack, one blank edge per card still to come (at most eight), with "Draw · 2 left" on the front edge. You draw by taking from the deck, which is what the game is. It fits "a beautiful boxed deck" literally.

Three and Twist go into the menu (vision: Three belongs next to the draw). A stack that thins out as you play is clear, but the front edge is a smaller tap target than today's gold bar. The gold main action becomes a gold word.

K · Small things up top

Spotlight's other rule, small items in the top corners, applied to the main screen: Three and Twist move up beside the logo, as in Spotlight. Under the card only one centred column remains: count, Spotlight, Next card with Back and Menu. The calmest of the five.

Three and Twist end up far from the card they change, at the top of the screen, which is hardest to reach with a thumb. The top corners are crowded with the logo.

Removed after round 1: the small diamond as separator or ornament (After Dark's, allowed inside buttons only), outlines around single controls, and corner brackets anywhere but on the card.
