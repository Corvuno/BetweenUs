Two sets of text

The same app three ways: as it is now, with a tight set, and with a loose set. Sizes, letter spacing, the creams and browns, the golds and the greens are brought down to a short list. Nothing moves, and no text gets smaller or darker.

"Now" already has the firmer small words and the metal line that are being built in another conversation, so you compare against where the app is going, not where it was this morning.

  • Tight: five creams and browns, one gold for words, one green. Green means only "you can tap this". The small explaining lines that were green ("the question takes the stage", "Change", "Shuffle") become brown, like other reading text.
  • Loose: seven creams and browns, both golds, and the green keeps one softer partner for those explaining lines.
  • In both, the faint ones become readable: "tap to change", the menu's "Change", "Shuffle" and "Off", the close cross, and the empty star on the card.
  • The solid gold of the Draw button, the lines, and every category and chapter colour are not touched.
