The switch

A long press on any category turns the lines on for every chapter at once; another long press turns them off. A tap still picks, a long press never does. The video is the whole thing on a loop, List and Small cards side by side; the soft disc is a pretend finger so you can see where the press is.

What the hold feels like

While the finger is down the category presses in a little and dims slowly, the way Next Card dims when you hold it at the end of a hand. When the hold is long enough, it comes back to full strength as the lines arrive. The hold is the app’s own hold time, so it feels like the other hold in the game.

When the lines come on, the names glide down to their new places, the chapter grows to its new height, and the lines fade in just after, once the names are nearly home. In Small cards the card faces, rails and corners ease in at the same time. The hint fades out the same way “· tap to change” does. Going off is the same in reverse, lines first, then the names glide back.

I did not add a separate held pause between the press and the lines: the slow dim already is the wait. Nothing is shown on the phone for the buzz a phone can give when the hold completes; that is a build detail.
