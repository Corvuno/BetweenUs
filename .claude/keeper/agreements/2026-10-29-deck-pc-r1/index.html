What each one is

  • Today. The sheet as it is now: a 640px column, scrolls inside.
  • Wider. One wide window. Presets on one line, chapters in two columns, they still unfold in place. Shuffle, Cards and Draw in a fixed footer.
  • Sideways. Presets across the top, chapters as a list on the left, the opened chapter’s categories in a column on the right. One chapter at a time, so Open all is gone.
  • Rethink: beside the card. The Deck as a panel docked beside the card instead of a window over it. The card stays in view. One chapter at a time, so Open all is gone.
