The owner's words: make the bar under "Between Us" "a bit more bold like these lines... So its more
like this lamp or building. Unapologetically there." References: two Art Deco divider sheets (stepped
bundles, every line ending on a dot, diamond or hexagon), a stepped-glass sconce and a streamline

becomes a thing"; what the lamp and house give is the boldness; the inner pair keeps the deck's
colour; the title stays the strongest thing ("Never as much. More weight is oke").
