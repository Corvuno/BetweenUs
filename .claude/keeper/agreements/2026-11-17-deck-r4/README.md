# The Deck, round 4: the foot (#183)

Owner on round 3: the line is chosen ("It fits the look better"). Wildcards and the fixed sets
are line items, not selectors: they do different things; metal look too. Shuffle, Cards (and
Three) stay in The Deck: usability first. Asked for a few looks for those settings that work with
the line. Only the foot is drawn, from Into the Deep down, with the app's own words.

Wildcards and Fixed Sets, the same in all three: a pair under Between Us and After Dark, a metal
bar on top that fills when on (Wildcards gold, Fixed Sets pale), Wildcards says On, Fixed Sets
opens with an arrow.

1. **Stations on the line.** The line carries on: each setting has a stretch of it, a name, its
   choices set large with the chosen one in gold metal, and its hint under it.
2. **The plate.** The three settings in one double-framed plate, a column each, the chosen value
   in gold metal between two short strokes. No room for the hints.
3. **Metal strips.** Each setting a strip cut into parts, the chosen part solid gold metal.

The Keeper's own pick: 1.
