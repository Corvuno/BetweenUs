Under the card, round 4

Built on round 3's M, the gold bar. The card keeps a real card's proportions. This round is only about where Menu belongs.

What Menu is

Menu holds two groups and one extra: Tonight (The Deck, Start over), Yours (Played, Favourites), and Ways to play. The Deck is also what "Selected deck" at the top already opens. You open Menu rarely, never while playing a card. So it belongs either with the deck (top) or with your hands (the count), and away from the thumb's every-turn path. Each option below puts it in one of those places and says why.

Kept from round 3 in every option: the gold bar (Spotlight · Next card · Three), Twist on the card across from the star, Back as an undo for a few seconds after a draw, the coloured line along the card's bottom edge with the count under it. Hint space is always reserved, so nothing moves when a hint goes. Tap a picture to open it full size.

Why each place

P · Menu with the deck

"Selected deck" already opens The Deck, the menu's first item. Here it opens the whole menu: one door at the top for the evening's deck and everything you kept. Nothing is added to the screen except the menu mark beside the words. The bottom is only for playing.

One tap further to The Deck (open the menu, then The Deck). The top is the hardest place to reach with a thumb, which is fine for something opened rarely, but it's a reach.

Q · Menu with the hand

Played, Favourites and Start over are all about your hands, and the count is the hand. So the count opens the menu, on the bar's centre line, close to the thumb. It adds no new element; the count gets a job.

The count is status and now also a button, so people may tap it expecting something about the count only. The Deck is about the deck, not the hand, so it fits less well here.

R · Menu on the centre line

Everything hangs on one vertical axis: count, gold bar, then Menu as its quiet end, below the play controls. It's on its own and clearly named, the easiest of the three to find.

It adds one more line under the bar, so the column grows. It's the closest to "put it somewhere below", and its only reason is the axis.
