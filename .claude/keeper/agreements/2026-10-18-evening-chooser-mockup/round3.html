The Deck sheet, round 3

Built from the live app (main commit a61443c): each frame is the real sheet, captured from the running app at phone size, with only the styling of the picked / not-picked marks changed in the options. Two options, each one look for everything you can pick on the sheet. No verdict here.

Read this about the frames. A real hand of Balanced would not have Past and Deep picked; the frames show Balanced picked and Past and Deep picked, as asked, so both kinds of mark can be seen. In the two options Colbert Questionnaire is also shown picked (the current frames have it unpicked, as the brief described), so the picked look of a Fixed Sets item can be judged. After Dark is shown with Carnal and Kink picked. Because the sheet is longer than one screen (as it already is today), each version is shown as three frames: top, scrolled down, and After Dark open.

Option A cream words, colour on the mark

Top of the sheet: the presets, then the chapters
Scrolled down: Into the Deep open (Past and Deep picked), Fixed Sets open, Shuffle and Cards
After Dark open (Carnal and Kink picked)

The look, everywhere. Every item is a cream word with a 7px dot in its own colour. Not picked: dim cream word (the categories' current .64 cream) and a hollow ring. Picked: full cream word, filled dot, 1px underline in the item's colour. Applies to the ten presets, every category, Colbert Questionnaire and The 36, and the Shuffle and Cards choices (their colour is celadon, the colour the app already uses for the chosen choice in that row).

What it changes.

  • Presets lose their coloured words and gain the same ring/dot as categories; the glow on the picked preset's dot is gone.
  • Categories keep the look they have today (this option is today's category look, extended).
  • Fixed Sets: items are now built exactly like category items (same dot, word, 44px height, 9px padding), and the panel has the same padding and border as a chapter panel, so Colbert and The 36 start at the same left edge as Past and Roots and the empty space under them is gone. The list glyph is replaced by the dot, in the set's colour.
  • Shuffle and Cards: two lines. The labels SHUFFLE and CARDS are in the row-label style of FIXED SETS (Jost, caps, same size and tone), in a label column; the choices sit beside them, each with the same mark. No Federo words in the row, no boxes. Choices stay 48px tall.
  • All items share one type size, 12.8px (the presets' current size; categories were 12px, Fixed Sets and Shuffle/Cards choices 12.5px) and the category spacing. Tap heights: presets 49px, categories and Fixed Sets 44px, Shuffle and Cards 48px, as today.

What might not work.

  • The hollow rings of the darkest colours (the After Dark ones, Shadow, World) are faint on the dark sheet, so for those the dot barely says "not picked" (this already holds for categories today).
  • Presets lose the colour they carry as words today; the colour is only on a small dot, so the preset row is quieter and the ten names read as one cream list.
  • The two-line Shuffle and Cards takes about 48px more height than one line; the sheet scrolls already.
  • Preset rows may wrap a little differently because the dot adds width (compare the top frame).

What it changes.

  • Categories lose the cream word and dot; they become coloured words, like the presets are today. A picked category is its full colour with a line under it.
  • Presets keep their coloured words; the dot (and its glow) goes, so presets and categories read the same way.
  • Fixed Sets: the same alignment fix as Option A (same item build, same panel padding as a chapter panel), words in the set's own colour. The list glyph is gone.
  • Shuffle and Cards: the same two-line redraw as Option A, choices in celadon, dimmed when not picked, full with a line when picked.
  • Same 12.8px type size and tap heights as Option A.

What might not work.

  • Picked versus not picked is a difference of brightness and an underline only; with colours that are already dim (the lightened ones, Mind, Family) the two states sit closer together than in Option A, where a filled dot says it.
  • Many colours at once make the sheet busier; the cream words of today are calmer and every colour is shown on a small mark instead.
  • The lightened colours are new colours for those categories on this sheet; if the owner says no, those words stay as dark as they are today and are hard to read (the After Dark ones most).
  • Two neighbours that share a hue (Past and Home, Culture and Quick) are told apart by colour alone.
