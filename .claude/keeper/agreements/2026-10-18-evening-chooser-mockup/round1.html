Every frame is a real 390 x 844 phone screen. The sheet is the one headed "What kind of evening?". Preset Balanced is selected everywhere, so it matches today's sheet. In the mix views, Carnal, Past, Connect and Deep are picked, as an example of the cross-chapter mix.

Option 1: Trimmed door: "Pick your own categories"

Changes. Today's sheet with the Tune section and both sliders removed, and Explore categories turned into a plain door at the bottom. The chapter rows and the preset chips are tightened in height so presets, chapters and Round fit one screen. The mix view replaces the sheet content in place: same sheet, the title changes to the door's wording, a Back link appears on the left, Draw Cards stays.

Keeps. Start from, Chapters, Round as headings, the two bordered Shuffle and Cards rails, every row exactly as today.

Might work: the smallest change, nothing new to learn, and the mix view is plainly a place you go to and come back from. Might not: the sheet still reads as three stacked lists, and the tightened rows have a smaller tap height than today (about 47px for chapters, 33px for presets).

Option 2: Two steps door: "Mix your own"

Changes. The sheet is read in order: 1 "Who's at the table" (presets), 2 "How far tonight goes" (chapters, with a line saying the preset already filled them), then Shuffle and Cards as one compact line with no boxes, then Draw Cards, then the door. The mix view slides in from the right over the evening, leaving a strip of the evening visible on the left, with "Back to the evening" at the top.

Keeps. Preset names, chapter names and lines, the title, Draw Cards. Chapters still toggle one by one as today.

Might work: the numbers say where to start and what is already done for you; the sliding panel makes the mix feel like a layer on top, not a path beside. Might not: the step labels and the hint line are new words for the owner to approve; it is still six rows of chapters.

Option 3: Rethink, the layers door: "Choose categories yourself"

Changes. Chapters are drawn as layers you descend, Arrive at the top to After Dark at the bottom, on one line running down the sheet. No row boxes. A lit layer is filled in its chapter colour; the sheet gets slightly darker towards the bottom. The mix view slides up from the bottom over the layers, and shows every category under the same descent line.

Behaviour change. Tapping a layer lights it and every layer above it ("go down to here"), instead of switching that one chapter on or off. A chapter in the middle can no longer be left out from this screen; that moves to the mix view. Presets that skip a layer (Balanced skips Arrive and Into the Deep) show their gaps as they open, and the first tap on a layer makes the run continuous (second frame).

Keeps. Preset names, chapter names and lines, the title, Draw Cards, the compact Round line, the After Dark spice mark.

Might work: depth is the real idea of the evening and this draws it, so "how far down" is one tap and the chapter order explains itself. Might not: it changes how chapters behave and breaks the "Balanced skips two layers" picture; players who like to switch one chapter off lose that here.
