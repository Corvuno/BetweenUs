Rules drawn. Tapping the chapter row (name and line) switches the whole chapter on or off. The fold target is the right-hand part of each row: the count (for example 1 / 5) over a celadon chevron, set off by a short vertical hairline, no box or pill. Chevron points down closed, up open. The left rail of each row is split into one segment per category: full when the chapter is fully on, some segments lit when only some are on, dim when off. A partly-on row also has half the background tint of a fully-on one and a name colour between on and off, so it reads in between.

Categories. Each is its own tap target, 44px tall. The word is always cream so every colour stays readable: picked is full cream, a filled dot and a thin line under the word, both in the exact category colour; unpicked is dimmer cream with a hollow ring in the exact category colour. I did not set the word itself in the category colour, because World, Shadow, Kink and the other dark ones cannot be read that way, picked or not.

None, not All. A None button sits at the right of the Chapters heading, where it clears every chapter and category so you can build a mix from nothing (the "Carnal, Past, Connect, Deep" evening). All is left out: the Everything preset already does that, and a second way to the same state is clutter. If Everything is not the same as all categories, that is something to check.

Custom. Once a mix no longer matches a preset the Start from row reads "Custom" in copper, as the app does today.

Fixed Sets. The last row in the list; Abyss and Magical stay out. The whole row is the fold (no on/off for the pair, since the two sets are played start to finish and do not mix); inside, Colbert Questionnaire and The 36 are picked one by one like categories.

Spice mark. The After Dark mark moves up next to the chapter name, since the right edge now holds the fold target. It is still its own control, drawn as today.
