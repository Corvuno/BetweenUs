# The line under the title takes the logo's centre — 2026-10-14

Approved by the owner on 2026-10-14, after mockup round 3
(https://claude.ai/artifact/PdChQUyAyQBEH7QQohioTu, option "B, lines centred").
Earlier rounds in this session were quick sketches sent as images.

## Why

The logo and the line under the title were meant to be family, but their centres looked
very different: the logo's two diamonds overlap deeply around a big filled centre, the
line's only touched around a small chip. The owner: the line is "a flourish, an ornament,
else it becomes a thing" — it echoes the logo, it doesn't compete with it. That is also why
the extra-line version (more bands, closest to the logo's structure) was turned down: liked,
but too much.

## B) Looks — what changes

- The line under the title (header ornament): the two outlined diamonds overlap the way the
  logo's do, so the filled centre becomes a clear diamond instead of a chip. The diamonds are
  grown (same overall width of the centre group), the outer gold bars move out with their
  top and bottom points, and the preset-coloured pair sits evenly between the gold bars and
  the centre line. Exactly as in round 3 "B, lines centred".
- The Spotlight ornament: same design, half of it — the new centre plus the right half,
  just like it is a half of the current line today. Its own colour (the card's) stays.

## What deliberately does not change

- Colours: gold bars, preset-coloured pair, copper centre on the line; the Spotlight
  ornament's card colour.
- The centres' colours stay different (line copper, start-card logo in the preset colour):
  "it didn't need to be the same, just same-ish".
- No extra lines.
- The logo itself (start card, app icon), the line's overall width, everything else on screen.

## How the areas touch

Looks only. No behaviour changes.
