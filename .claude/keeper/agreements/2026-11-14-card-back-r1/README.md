Reference: `card-frame-gold-lines.jpg`. Owner before this round, on what pulls in it: "It feels like
the ultimate clash of deco elements. I like that." On today's back: "It's not wrong. This is nicer to
look at." "Lattice may go." The double frame stays as the outer edge "if that works. Yes if it
interferes it goes."

`backs.js` draws in a field that fills the frame's inner line; the logo, the short line and the
words in the window are the app's own.

- `bands`: the reference laid on its side and drawn new. Bundles of lines cut the corners and cross
  bundles that run into a window with cut corners; a woven diamond at each end; stepped bars with a
  round piece top and bottom; a ringed dot in each corner.
- `sunburst`: rays from behind a window with stepped corners, a double ring through them, a stepped
  tower top and bottom, a fan at each end, stepped blocks in the corners.

Nothing here is chosen. How the back arrives at the end of a hand is not designed in this round.
