owner says yes to the whole agreement.** Saved so the decisions so far survive a lost

## Standing rule agreed (2026-10-13)

Every category this work touches gets two descriptions:
- an app line (tagline) — short, in the app;
- a writer brief — what the category is about, the subjects a card may cover, and where
  it stops. Lives in `questions/between-us-deck.md`, not in the app.
The tagline alone always ends up too narrow and makes cards drift to sameness.

## Decided so far

**Life + Home split.** Home = the place (chair to town, why there, who's in it, where
next). Life = what you do there: ordinary daily life, everything that has to be done.
Example: "why you live in this city" is Home; "what your Sunday chores look like" is Life.
Writer briefs for both: owner likes the subjects, instructions and boundaries as drafted
(chat 2026-10-13). Life app line: not chosen yet.

**Home app line:** "Your rooms, your street, your town — and the next one".
Keep: Home 4 (which chair/corner), Home 8 (kitchen window). Home 2/5/6 (belonging) and
7/9 (home reveals you) agreed bad. Home 11 sits on the Roots border — settle at card stage.

acceptable without being After Dark. Tagline direction: the old "Wanting, and being

sexually. Desire — the edge of After Dark: just acceptable without being After Dark.
