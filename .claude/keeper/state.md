# Where Between Us stands

The overview every conversation starts from. One screen; what is no longer true is removed.
Backlog items are named by number and title.

## Strong

- The line under the title and the logo: the owner calls them awesome. They are the source of
  the look, and they came from many references.
- The card back, the richest object in the app; new backs are chosen (#175).
- The card face: the category named at the top with its stripes, in a double frame with the
  category's colour in the inner line. The owner chose it from pictures of the live app.
  The start card, the Intermezzo and Spotlight wear the same frame.
- The deck: every card has a number and a slot, the long cards are shortened, categories sit in
  pairs. The owner's ear for cards is sharp and recorded in `taste.md`.
- The Deck sheet and its chapters work.

## Weak, in the owner's words

- Design overall: "takes rounds and rounds, and the end result is almost always mediocre".
  Controls and the computer layout are the hardest.
- The bottom of the phone screen: "the way it works. It's terrible"; "all muted and all over the
  place". The hairlines beside Next card irk most: generic, with no purpose but to flank a button.
  Every piece there must have a job. Below the controls a third of the screen is empty.
- The title: "very thin and not there". The font stays.
- The computer's card screen has its earlier layout back (Back, and Three, Spotlight, Twist top
  right); the owner calls it good enough until "a full design pass".

## What the owner wants next, in their order

1. The title firmer, in its own font: three strengths shown, waiting for a pick (#169).
2. The bottom of the phone screen, with a new idea (#160): drawn next, in the weight now live.
3. The balance of the card screen on a computer (#161), as the full design pass.
Around these, ready in `Next`: Save, Clear and Start over say what they did (#158)
and The card follows the finger, and skip goes (#165), in that order; Plain words for The Deck's
settings (#157); a row of accessibility fixes in `Next`. The sideways phone (#156) and the
website (#71) are parked: the owner never turns the phone, and the address comes later.

## Open with the owner

- The text set (#148) has landed: the owner looks once on their own phone at "Selected deck",
  "tap to change", "Balanced" and "Start from". On a computer some category lines in The Deck
  now take two lines (Jost is wider than Federo was).
- A design mood board for Between Us, starting with controls and the PC layout (#147): in its
  own conversation; it opens by asking what the board should help decide.
- What makes a design enjoyable for them, said about the card back and true for every design:
  the balance of lines and black, small flourishes that emerge from the lines, areas cut out
  with their own design, lines that lead the eye, playfulness. Turned down: clean, predictable,
  safe, too even, empty space in random places.
- New card backs (#175) are chosen and ready in `Next`, now that the text styles have landed: petrol
  streams on the start card, amethyst circles on the Intermezzo. What got there: references as
  lessons, never redrawn; lines that never float, fine and quiet, full edge to edge; a window whose
  shape the drawing makes; several really different options at once ("If you give me one, ill be
  shooting it down all day"). Petrol and amethyst arrive as the backs' colours; the app's accent
  colour in the vision is still open.
- Designing the card face went well when rough sketches on real cards came first and the owner
  could name the parts they liked; it went wrong when a liked part came back reshaped.
- Weight: the middle ground is live (small words one step firmer, metal lines on The Deck and the
  menu, the card's coloured line at four fifths). More than that "lost the elegance".
- Testing: a sample is enough, and what works is left alone. The owner judges by using it.

- Whether very long questions still run off the card on a phone was closed without a test.
