# The card panel

A panel of player characters that read cards and say how each one lands for them. The idea
came from a former colleague of the owner who runs panels of stakeholder characters (the
enthusiast, the data person, the sales guy) to give feedback on innovation proposals.
First test 2026-10-27, on the 64 cards from rework rounds 2–5. The owner: "So it helps."

The panel gives feedback only. It never approves a card, never proposes card text, and every
card still goes to the owner word for word.

## The characters

1. **The plain talker**: reads once and has to know what to say. Flags abstract, double-barrelled
   or unclear cards.
2. **The light one**: here for fun, won't dig. Flags homework cards; checks that a card still works
   with a quick surface answer.
3. **The overthinker**: finds the second reading. Flags ambiguity and borrowed devices (dating-app
   prompts, party-game setups).
4. **The one whose life doesn't fit**: small friend group (at most four), few childhood memories,
   single, not much money, no kids. Flags cards that assume a life they don't have.
5. **The stranger in the queue**: has known the table ten minutes. Flags cards that go too far,
   too fast for their category, and After Dark cards that turn giggly.

## Calibration (what the panel must not flag)

- **2026-10-28, owner:** everyone has grandparents, and has at least heard stories about them.
  A card about grandparents is not a "life doesn't fit" problem; that the rare orphan can't
  answer is accepted.
- **2026-10-28, Keeper (after the first test):** the stranger judges a card against its own
  category's depth band, not against a table of strangers. Deep, Raw, Shadow and Grief are
  heavy on purpose; who sits at the table is handled by the presets.
- **2026-10-28, Keeper:** the overthinker only raises a second reading when the two readings
  would make people answer differently. "Which family?" or "how little?" is not a finding.
- **2026-10-28, Keeper:** in the first test, voices wore down to one word after about 40 cards.
  Run the panel in batches of about 20 cards, or one agent per character.

## Runs

- **2026-10-27** — rounds 2–5, 64 cards: 46 keep, 16 look again, 2 weak (Friendship 12, Self 8).
  The owner agreed with the panel on Wish 6 (too light) and Wish 8 (vague).
