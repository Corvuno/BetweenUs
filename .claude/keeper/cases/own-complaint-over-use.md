# A redesign chased the Keeper's own complaint

Resolved.

- What happened: in a review the Keeper called The Deck "the one place that feels like software".
  The owner asked for better plans out of curiosity. The Keeper argued the item closed, then drew
  rounds aimed at its own complaint, and finally proposed moving Shuffle and Cards out of The Deck
  to make it feel less like settings.
- What the owner expected: a better design for how The Deck is used. "You're just looking for a
  way to solve your issue. Not usability. That's number one." Asked whether a design solved the
  complaint, the honest answer was no, and the owner had never shared the complaint.
- Cause: the Keeper treated its own reading of a screen as the brief, and judged designs by it
  instead of by what the owner and players do there.
- Changed: `.claude/skills/keeper/design.md` lives in the Keeper repository; the product-side
  lesson is in `look.md`, "Controls": usability decides, and a complaint of the Keeper's own is
  a question for the owner, not a brief.
