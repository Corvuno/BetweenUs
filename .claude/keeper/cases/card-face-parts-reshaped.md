Resolved.

  another and the colour of a third, and asked for them combined. The Keeper merged name and frame
  into one object (the name in a gap of the frame) and took "the colour" to be the glow.

  they had turned down, and the glow as contrived.
- Cause: the Keeper redesigned liked parts to make them fit, and guessed which part of a sketch
  "the colour" was, where a few variants would have asked it.
