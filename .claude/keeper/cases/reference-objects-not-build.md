# A reference was used for its objects, not for how it is built

Resolved.

- What happened: asked to take the card back towards the gold-line card frame, the Keeper drew
  two backs from the frame's objects (diamonds, circles, fans, rays) and then three variations of
  one that differed only in how many lines they had.
- What the owner expected: the frame's character. "Too little cohesion, too many different
  objects without relations"; "look at the back of the card I said I like and look at your work.
  What do you notice?"
- Cause: the Keeper read the reference as a list of ornaments instead of studying how it is drawn:
  one thin weight, lines in bundles of parallels with narrow even channels, bundles that run along
  the edges and cross, ornament only where lines meet, an empty middle that is the shape the lines
  leave. The round of three variations broke `design.md`'s line that options differing only in
  detail are one option.
- Changed: `look.md`, "Where the look comes from": what adapting a reference faithfully means.
