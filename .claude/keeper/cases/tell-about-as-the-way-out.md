# "Tell about" became the way out of "What"

Resolved.

- What happened: asked for cards that ask in more ways than what, which, who and when, the Keeper
  reached for "Tell about…" in almost every slot as the alternative that isn't a question.
- What the owner expected: cards that invite. "The tell is your new favourite getaway from the what.
  It's beginning to get boring… it shouldn't be 'do', it should be an invite." Taking the words out
  gives a better card nearly every time.
- Cause: the card notes listed "Tell about…" first among the forms that aren't a question, so it
  read as the default.
- Changed: `cards.md` (forms that aren't a question), `taste.md` ("A card invites; it doesn't
  order", and the device list), and house style 13 in the deck notes.
