# A depth band argued as if it were fixed

Resolved.

- What happened: Past was drifting lighter in the card audit. The Keeper said Past "shouldn't
  become light, because its band is 6 too", and chose cards to hold it there.
- What the owner expected: the band to be questioned as freely as a card. "It is not a given that
  Past is 6… We gave it a number because it was then that number." Nothing is set in stone unless
  the owner says so; a better fix may be moving the band, the chapter, or cards between Past and
  Roots.
- Cause: `cards.md` named both ways out (the card goes or the band moves), but the Keeper treated
  the current number as the standard to defend.
- Changed: `cards.md`, the band line now says a band, chapter or border is where things stood when
  set, and moving them is as open as changing a card. The generic `owner.md` needs the same line
  ("nothing is set in stone"); it lives in the Keeper repository, which this session could not
  reach.
