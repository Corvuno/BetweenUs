# Vision — Between Us

Where the product is going and who it is for. It gives direction; how a screen is laid out today
lives in `design/` and `state.md`, and changes freely.

## What it is

A way into another person. Between Us lets a conversation go where it rarely gets to on its own,
in steps: warm up, go further, come back to land, the way a good evening does. Or fully random.

A card brings up something about the person: who they are, what they want, how they think. A
story, a confession, a position, a theory about themselves, or a big open question a table can
argue about. The best cards catch the gap between who someone thinks they are and what they
actually do. Desire and darkness belong to people, so they belong in the deck, in grown-up words
and never juvenile.

What it leaves behind: surprise at someone else, sometimes at yourself. "I'd never have guessed
that about you", or better, "I didn't even know this about myself". That works at ten minutes or
at ten years.

## Who it's for

Anyone curious about people: friends, colleagues, dates, strangers. In a queue, in a car on the
way home, at a team day, with one friend or eight. The deck adjusts to how many play and who's at
the table.

Someone finding it on their own, from a post or a link, should meet a beautiful boxed deck:
clearly made for an evening with other people, and exciting to look through alone.

## How it feels to use

- You open it and draw. Everything else is there to discover once you're curious.
- The question is the point. The screen is about the card in front of you; what isn't needed to
  play steps back.
- It feels like one deck on every device, phone, computer or full screen, each shaped for its
  screen.
- A player draws a card and it is theirs. Nothing is dealt, and the words stay clear of gambling
  and card tables.

## How it looks

Art deco: geometry, symmetry, fine line work. Pretty and elegant, and bold. Modern touches are
welcome, and a piece may simply be pretty without ornament, as long as the whole holds together.
Everything is made for this deck rather than taken from stock.

## Where it's going

Its own website, free, in English, shared by the owner wherever people gather to find good things.
The deck, the looks and the app grow side by side:

- **Deck:** every card earns its slot, nothing repeats, every category stays wide enough to be
  fun.
- **Looks:** good enough to release and show off.
- **App:** as smooth as the cards.

Later doors, kept open: a printed deck, which could also bring in money, and an offshoot with no
shape yet.
