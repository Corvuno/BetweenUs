# Be Free rave wall

A side project, parked for reference; not part of Between Us and not read at the start of a session.

- The wall: open, uplifting intentions in a few words ("fully let go", "connect with others", "find
  peace", "see the beauty of things").
- Prompts liked: "Today I give myself permission to…", "If I were my own best friend…", "This
  morning my body is asking me to…", "A wish for myself, for today…".
- Turned down: prompts asking for specific objects, places, body parts or skills, and "What I'm
  leaving on the dance floor…" (it's about what you get there).
- Posters: the co-brand option, "clearly both".
