# Notes for the looks builder

Corrections from earlier jobs. Read all of them before starting; they weigh as much as your
hard rules. The Keeper adds to this file and tidies it monthly. Newest at the bottom.

Format: **date** — what happened / what to do instead.

## Notes

- **2026-10-12** — The owner: "the word deal does not exist." / Players *draw* a card (they take it, it's theirs); nothing is dealt or handed out, and no gambling or card-table words. Use "draw" for every card action, in copy, mockups, comments and names alike. (from lesson 2026-10-12)
- **2026-10-13** — A help line talked down to the player ("all the little tips for first-timers"). / Copy in this app never treats the player as a beginner: no "little", no "tips for first-timers". Write as one adult to another — say who it's for or what it does. (from lesson 2026-10-13)
- **2026-10-13** — Controls got diamond outlines after the owner said "no loose pills and boxes". / No outline or container around a single control, whatever its shape; a control earns its tappability from colour, symbol, placement and line work. (from lesson 2026-10-13)
- **2026-10-13** — Three mockups that each tidied the current layout. / Every mockup round has at least one direction that rethinks the area, not only cleaned-up versions. (from lesson 2026-10-13)
- **2026-10-13** — Nicer looks for a wish whose point was "unclear to people". / When the goal is clarity, design for what makes it clear (naming, behaviour, placement) first; looks follow. (from lesson 2026-10-13)
- **2026-10-14** — A colour mockup used a blue preset and a blue card, so two colours matched by accident. / In any colour mockup, pick a preset and a card whose colours differ from each other and from the chrome being judged, and say which ones you used. (from lesson 2026-10-14)
