# Between Us: the rulebook

The owner's own words, section by section. Every design and every build follows this file.
Where it says nothing, the design finds the answer. Today's named values are in
`DESIGN-SYSTEM.md`; this file wins where the two differ.

## Overview

An elegant, desirable Art Deco question card game that brings people together.

How it should feel: "A good way to start the evening, asking questions. You should be drawn into
this space of luxury Art Deco saloon nights: not the Western, the Victorian style, or the later
even. Depth, and something of ease."

The problem this rulebook is for: "I like most elements of the game. The problem is it looks
incohesive. It should look more the same, but in another way."

## Colors

"Gold. Black and grey are in there. Dark golds, light golds, cream. The purple and the teal are
there too, and the category and chapter colours. So there are a lot of colours."

"They never show up all at once. They are reserved for the categories and the chapters; two of
them fill two colour spots. So it's never a lot of colours on the screen. It's always two distinct
colours that show you what you have activated, which I like because that gives it colour and it
changes a bit while you play. It makes the game feel alive and not dull. So that should absolutely
stay."

## Typography

Fixed, not up for discussion: the title in Poiret One, the question in Bodoni Moda, and the
category header above the question.

The fonts in the app: Poiret One, Bodoni Moda, Federo and Jost.

"The biggest thing is the title, then the question, then the category, and then the rest of things.
There are a few headings."

## Layout

"Centre composed. A clear hierarchy, centred around the question, which is the main purpose of the
game. On PC the card should be the largest thing; on the phone, a card in the middle."

Also fixed: the card's shape, and its top line in the category colour.

## Elevation & Depth

"Like the back of a card. Recognisable from far and pretty up close."

## Shapes

"Art Deco shapes. A lot of graphical, straight lines and bent lines. Round next to straight. Any Art
Deco shape could be."

## Components

"A title with a graphic underneath it. A way to select chapter. A card. A way to select next card. A
way to get into Menu and Spotlight. And the Menu, where the deck has space for the presets that draw
a number of cards, the chapters that hold categories that hold cards, and the way it shuffles."

## Do's and Don'ts

"Boring design. AI design. Buttons in any way, shape or form, like the pills, the edges around
everything. Clickable things should not look like the classical interface button. No glows, no film
grain, no highlights with a ray on top of them. No things that are unnecessary. No fake elements."
