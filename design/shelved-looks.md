# Shelved looks

Looks the owner liked but took out of their first place, kept to reuse somewhere else. Each says
what it is, where it came from and what the owner said. Pictures are in `design/shelved/`.

## The rail (was About, the You–Us lean in The Deck)

The chapter line turned on its side: two `--border-strong` rails 5px apart, a 22px gold mark
through the middle, a gold mark under each of the other eight steps, longer towards the ends
(3px plus 1.5px a step), and a 12px celadon diamond with a 3px `--surface` halo that slides over
nine steps. The stretch from the middle to the diamond fills with celadon metal (celadon mixed
50% into black at both ends, 45% into white in the middle), the way a chapter fills as its
categories come on. The word on the side it leans to takes the picked look; both words keep the
width of a bold YOU so nothing moves. An invisible range input with a 24px thumb lies over it.
Picture: `shelved/lean-rail.png` (the drawing from round 1; the built one was the same).

Owner: loved the look; "it works, maybe for somewhere else". It was replaced by the stops because
"it's not too much of a graphic, while I like that, it's not what it's supposed to mean": the rail
reads as a picture rather than as a setting with steps. It fits a place that shows an amount or a
share (a fill that grows), where a picture of how much is the meaning.

## The rings (round 1's rethink for the You–Us lean)

Two overlapping rings, You and Us. Tap a ring to lean towards it, tap where they overlap to go back
to the middle; the ring you lean to gets the heavier metal line and a soft celadon fill, the overlap
lights as the shared part, a small gold diamond sits at the top of the overlap. Five steps instead
of nine. Picture: `shelved/lean-rings.png`.

Owner: "I love the look of the rings. Really cool. Though way too complex for what it does"; "save
it for later, might come in handy". It fits a place where two people or two sides are the subject
itself, not a setting.
