# The bar under the name

The ornament between "Between Us" and the tagline (`.title-rule`, `.tr-svg` in `between-us.html`; its
look in `styles.css`, "The bar under the name"). It is where the app's look comes from, and its centre
is the logo: the two overlapping diamonds, which are "between us", with the copper diamond where they
overlap.

## What the owner settled

- It is "unapologetically there", in the spirit of their references: two Art Deco divider sheets, a
  stepped-glass sconce and a streamline house (`.claude/keeper/references/`, sconce and streamline
  entries). The weight comes from bundles of lines, steps and solid ends, not from thick strokes:
  "make the lines a bit less thick. They have room like this."
- The name stays the strongest thing at the top: "Never as much. More weight is oke".
- The inner pair keeps the colour of the picked deck.
- The owner chose round 2, option 2, "a full ornament": lines grow out of the diamonds in steps, the
  nearer the middle the further they reach; the gold bars are solid bands with rounded ends off the
  diamonds' tips; a fine gold line, the deck-coloured pair, and the middle line reaching furthest;
  every fine line stops on a dot, the middle line on a ring with a dot. Nothing fades.

## How it is built

- One drawing for phone and computer; on a computer it is wider and the line weights stay the same.
- The drawing's box is cropped to the bands, so the header grows only by what the ornament adds and a
  360 by 640 phone still fits without scrolling.
- Small diamonds stay off the line (they belong to After Dark); the logo is the only diamond in it.
- The rounds, with the other options (streamline bands, stepped glass, the name standing on the bar),
  are in `.claude/keeper/agreements/title-bar-r1` and `title-bar-r2`.
