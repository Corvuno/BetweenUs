# The Deck sheet heading

The evening chooser sheet (`#cat-area`, aria-label "The Deck") is named **The Deck**: a
`.drawer-title` (the same heading every drawer and panel uses: Jost, uppercase, `.16em` tracking,
`--gold`, centred; .75rem, .85rem from 1024px, .9rem from 1180px) with `.cat-sheet-name` (6px
under it, no other value). The time-of-day question (`#playLeadFirst`, "What kind of evening?",
set by `updateTimeOfDayHeading`) stays as the line directly under it, in its quieter tone (cream at
.6, Federo, sentence case). Close (x) is unchanged. No new box, line or glow. Reason: the sheet
needs a name, not only a question.

The card screen's label reads "Selected deck"; the " · tap to change" / "click to change" hint
stays (`--muted`).
