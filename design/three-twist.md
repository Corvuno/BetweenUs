# Three and Twist

- Three is a setting in The Deck (`#pickToggle`, Off / On, the same picked / not-picked look as
  Shuffle and Cards, `.three-opt`), in the settings lines at the end of the chapters list, above
  Draw Cards (each on its own line on a computer).
- Twist is a line in the Menu sheet, in the menu's own row style, dimmed (opacity .32) when no card
  is in front of you.
- Neither has a button on the main screen or in Spotlight; keys 3 and T still work in Spotlight,
  and Twist's sentence still shows there.
